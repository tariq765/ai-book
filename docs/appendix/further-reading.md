## Research Papers (Key Milestones)

### Transformers & LLMs
- **Attention Is All You Need** (Vaswani et al., 2017) — Original transformer paper
- **BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers** (Devlin et al., 2018)
- **Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3)** (Brown et al., 2020)
- **Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback** (Ouyang et al., 2022) — InstructGPT
- **LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models** (Touvron et al., 2023)

### RAG & Retrieval
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks** (Lewis et al., 2020) — Original RAG
- **Dense Passage Retrieval for Open-Domain Question Answering** (Karpukhin et al., 2020) — DPR
- **REPLUG: Retrieval-Augmented Black-Box Language Models** (Shi et al., 2023)

### Agents & Tool Use
- **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models** (Yao et al., 2022)
- **Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools** (Schick et al., 2023)
- **Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior** (Park et al., 2023)

### Evaluation
- **Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code** (Chen et al., 2021) — HumanEval
- **Holistic Evaluation of Language Models** (Liang et al., 2022) — HELM
