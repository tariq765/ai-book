# Part I: Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

**Chapters 1–4**

---

## Overview

Before diving into modern AI, we need a solid foundation. This part covers the fundamental concepts that everything else builds upon.

## Chapters

| Chapter | Title | Key Concepts |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | [What Is Artificial Intelligence?](/foundations/chapter-1-what-is-ai) | AI definition, history, narrow vs general AI, applications |
| 2 | [Types of Artificial Intelligence](/foundations/chapter-2-types-of-ai) | Narrow AI, Generative AI, Predictive AI, Agentic AI, Multimodal AI |
| 3 | [Machine Learning](/foundations/chapter-3-machine-learning) | Supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement learning, training, validation, testing |
| 4 | [Deep Learning and Neural Networks](/foundations/chapter-4-deep-learning-neural-networks) | Neurons, layers, backpropagation, activation functions |

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:

- Understand what AI is and how it differs from traditional programming
- Distinguish between different types of AI systems
- Grasp the machine learning lifecycle
- Understand neural network architecture and training

## Prerequisites

- Basic programming knowledge (Python recommended)
- High school mathematics (algebra, basic calculus concepts)
