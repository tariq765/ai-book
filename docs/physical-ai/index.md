# Part VIII: Physical AI

**Chapters 27–29**

---

## Overview

Physical AI bridges the gap between digital intelligence and the physical world. This part covers computer vision, robotics, and humanoid robotics.

## Chapters

| Chapter | Title | Key Concepts |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| 27 | [Computer Vision](/physical-ai/chapter-27-computer-vision) | Image classification, object detection, segmentation, vision-language models |
| 28 | [Robotics and Physical AI](/physical-ai/chapter-28-robotics-physical-ai) | Sensors, perception, planning, control, simulation, human-robot interaction |
| 29 | [Humanoid Robotics](/physical-ai/chapter-29-humanoid-robotics) | Actuators, motion planning, embodied intelligence, simulation |

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:

- Understand computer vision fundamentals and modern architectures
- Grasp robotics perception, planning, and control loops
- Understand humanoid robot architecture and challenges
- See how AI connects to physical embodiment

## Prerequisites

- Parts I-IV (neural networks, agents)
- Python programming
- Basic physics/mechanics concepts (explained in context)
